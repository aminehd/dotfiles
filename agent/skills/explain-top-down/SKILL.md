---
name: explain-top-down
description: Use when I ask what some code, YAML, chart or system does, or how it works.
---

1. One sentence: what it is for and who uses it.
2. The main parts and how data or control moves between them. A small ASCII diagram if useful.
3. Trace one real example through it, step by step, citing path:line for each step.
4. The one surprising or important detail.
5. What I would look at next to go deeper.
Keep it short. Skip parts that do not matter for my question.
